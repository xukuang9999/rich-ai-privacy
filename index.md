# Rich AI — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 20 October 2026_

Rich AI is a personal assistant app for restaurant and café owners, published by Kuang Xu ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## Summary

- Rich AI has **no accounts** and **no sign-in**.
- Your records are stored **on your device**.
- When you allow cloud AI, the content needed for a request is sent to the AI provider that answers it. Nothing is sold, and nothing is used for advertising or tracking.

## Data stored on your device

The app keeps everything you create on your iPhone or iPad: meetings (recordings, transcripts and notes), reminders and tasks, spending and budgets, sites and site photos, menu concepts and recipes, designs and renders, inspiration photos, ROI scenarios and assistant chats. You can edit or delete any record in the app, and deleting the app removes its data from the device. Backups you export are files you control.

## Data sent to service providers

Cloud AI features are optional. The first time you use one, the app asks for permission, and you can turn cloud AI off at any time in **Settings**. When it is on:

- **Anthropic (Claude)** receives the text, photos, receipts, transcripts and record details needed to answer your request — for example a chat message and the records it refers to, a meeting transcript to summarise, or a site's address and photos for research. Site research may include the site's location.
- **OpenAI** receives meeting audio for cloud transcription and design prompts or photos for image renders, only if you turn those features on.
- **A Live voice service** receives your voice and typed messages during a voice conversation, only if one has been set up in Settings.
- **Apple** services are used for on-device speech recognition, Apple Intelligence, maps and address search, under Apple's own privacy policy.

These providers process the data to return a result to you. Their handling of the data is governed by their own terms and privacy policies:
[Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy) · [OpenAI](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy) · [Apple](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Permissions

The app asks for the microphone (recording meetings and voice chat), speech recognition (on-device transcription), camera and photos (site, receipt and inspiration photos), location while in use (filling in a site's address), Face ID (optional app lock), calendar (adding an event you create) and notifications (reminders). Each permission is used only for the feature that asks for it, and you can change it in iOS Settings.

## Tracking and analytics

Rich AI does not track you across apps or websites, does not use advertising identifiers and does not include third-party analytics.

## Children

Rich AI is not directed to children under 13.

## TestFlight

If you install a beta through TestFlight, Apple shares crash reports and the feedback you choose to send with us, as described in Apple's TestFlight terms.
